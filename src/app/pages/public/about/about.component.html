About Us

Cloth Charity is dedicated to spreading warmth and hope by collecting and distributing clothes to those in need.

Our Mission

We aim to ensure that no one suffers from the cold due to lack of clothing. Our mission is to connect donors and volunteers with communities in need.

Our Vision

We envision a world where compassion drives action, and every individual has access to basic clothing and dignity.

Our Values

Compassion, transparency, community-driven projects, and empowering volunteers to be agents of change.

Our Journey

From our first winter drive in 2020 to large-scale community distributions in 2025, our journey reflects the power of collective kindness.

Our Impact

We have distributed over 10,000 clothing items, organized 15+ charity events, and built a volunteer network of 500+ active members.
